Quick Rules

Goal: Score the most points by collecting colors into your River and Cup.

On your turn, play a card from your hand to either:

• A Mountain (shared center) — adds a new color to the mandala

• A Field (your side) — commits cards of a color already in the mountain

• Discard a card to draw a new one

When a mandala has all 6 colors, it closes and enters the Claim phase. The player with more field cards picks first, choosing one color at a time from the mountain. Players alternate picks until all colors are claimed.

The first card of each claimed color goes to your River (sets scoring order), the rest to your Cup (worth points). Colors earlier in your River are worth more points.
